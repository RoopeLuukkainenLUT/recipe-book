# Mudcake recipe
**`NOTE`** for this assignment I used an exising recipe, since I am not able to bake/cook anything without recipe made by someone else. I have used this one earlier and translated it to English. Recipe is from K-Ruoka [Helppo mutakakku](https://www.k-ruoka.fi/reseptit/helppo-mutakakku)

## Preparation

- Buy ingredients
- Own a cake tin with removable bottom (e.g. ⌀ 24 cm)
- Own an oven

## Ingredients

These use SI-units
- 150g butter (for more healthy option use baking margarine)
- 2dl sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1.5dl wheat flour 
- 3/4dl cocoa powder
- 2 tsp vanilla sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder


## Instructions

- Attach the baking paper to the bottom of the cake tin (⌀ 24 cm).
- Melt the butter with low heat in a saucepan or microwave.
- Mix the sugar with the fat. Add the eggs.
- Mix dry ingredients. Add them (flour mixture) to the dough. Mix only enough to make the dough even. Spread the dough on the cake tin.
- Bake (instructions below)
- Serve the mud cake with whipped cream or vanilla ice cream.

## Baking

- 12-15 min at 200 °C
- Cake should remain a little raw from the middle
